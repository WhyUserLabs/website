Common questions

Answers, briefly.

What does an ad campaign test cover?

LinkedIn and Google ads, from the feed to the page they open. Each role sees the ad, clicks or scrolls past, then lands on the page and converts or leaves. You can test two creatives side by side.

What does it cost?

50 credits a creative, with 300 simulated buyers each, on the Scale plan and above. About 30 minutes. Pricing.

Are the click and conversion numbers real rates?

No. Simulated buyers click and convert far more often than real ones, so each role is judged against an average ad in the same simulation. Treat the result as direction, never as a forecast CTR.

Can it tell me which creative wins?

It ranks the creatives and says when they are too close to call. In the sample run the two creatives were tied, and the page, not the ad, was the problem.

Why test the page with the ad?

Because the click is sold on a promise. If the page does not keep it, the best creative still loses. WhyUser scores the ad and the page apart, so you know which one to fix.
