Common questions

Answers, briefly.

What does Audience Discovery return?

LinkedIn audiences ranked by seniority and job function, and by title family, each with how many simulated buyers were interested. It also maps every passing buyer by subvertical and committee seat, lists the buying triggers, and shows what the page lacks.

What does it cost?

50 credits a run, with 50 to 300 candidate buyers depending on the market. It is included on every plan. Pricing.

Can I check the titles I already target?

Yes. Paste the titles you sell to, and each one is read against the page by the same simulation the run used.

How is this different from LinkedIn’s own audience tools?

LinkedIn tells you who an audience is. WhyUser tells you which of them your page actually lands with, and why the rest are not ready, before you spend on impressions.

Are the interest numbers a forecast?

No. They are counts of simulated buyers, with a confidence range. Use them to choose and order audiences, then let your live campaign confirm the ranking.
