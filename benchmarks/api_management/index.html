How this was measured

A simulated buying committee read each homepage: the VP of Engineering, Staff Engineer, Engineering Manager, Backend Engineer, Security Engineer. Each read it three ways: skeptical, distracted and ideal. Counted findings are page facts anyone can check. Simulated findings are reported as runs, never as traffic.

Method details
Runs
810: 54 pages, five roles, three states each.
Motion
Each page was read under the directive its own motion called for: 28 self-serve, 26 enterprise.
