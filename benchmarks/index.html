  1. One capture dateA real browser opens each homepage at desktop size, closes the cookie banner and scrolls the whole page. “First screen” means the top 900 pixels.
  2. A yes needs a quoteEvery yes rests on a line copied word for word from the page, and code confirms the line is there. Nothing is simulated or estimated.
  3. Every row is publicEach company has its own row with the line behind every yes. Our own customers and design partners are left out.
  4. CorrectionsIf we misread your page, tell us. We recheck it and fix the row.
