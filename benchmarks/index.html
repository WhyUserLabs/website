How we measure

The same committee and the same checks on every page

  • Five readers. The person who signs, the technical decision maker, the champion, the person who uses it every day, and security. Each category gets its own job titles for them.
  • Three ways of reading. Each reader goes through the page skeptical, distracted and ideal.
  • Counted and simulated, kept apart. Counted findings are page facts anyone can check. Simulated findings are reported as runs, never as traffic.
  • Anonymous findings. Every company is named in the roster and anonymous in every chart. A company’s own row goes only to an address at that company.
  • Corrections. If we misread your page, tell us. We re-run it and publish the difference.

How the committee itself works: Committee Simulation. How often its predictions hold up: Accuracy.
