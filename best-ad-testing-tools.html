How to choose

Four questions pick the tool.

Who decides the purchase?

Consumers: attention AI and consumer panels fit. A B2B committee: test every role, not a typical consumer.

Where do clicks go?

If the ad leads to a page, test them together. The page is where most B2B clicks are lost.

Can you afford to learn live?

LinkedIn suggests $5,000 and three weeks per A/B test. Test before you spend, then A/B test the close calls.

Do you need human words?

For quotes, use a real panel. For direction and fixes across many versions, simulate.

Use them together

Simulate the funnel. Then spend.

  1. SimulateRun each ad and its page past the buying committee. Fix the page.
  2. AskShow the final creative to real buyers if you need quotes.
  3. LaunchSpend on the version that survived, with a page that keeps its promise.
  4. A/B testSettle the close calls in Campaign Manager.
Common questions

Answers, briefly.

What's the best tool for testing ad creative before launch?

For B2B ads, WhyUser: it tests each ad and its landing page together on 300 simulated buyers across five roles, and shows who clicks, who leaves at the page and why, in about 30 minutes. For real B2B reactions, Wynter. For attention on the image, Neurons. After launch, a LinkedIn A/B test settles the close calls.

Which AI ad testing tools actually work for business audiences?

Most AI ad testing tools, such as Kantar LINK AI, Neurons and Dragonfly AI, are built on consumer data or visual attention. WhyUser builds B2B buyers from reviews, community threads, buyer questions and your calls, and runs them as a buying committee of five roles.

How do the top ad testing platforms compare on price?

PickFu starts at $1 a response. Wynter starts at $899 a test. Zappi lists about $2,500 to $5,000 per solution, and Motion starts at $999 a month. Neurons, System1 and Dragonfly AI quote on request. LinkedIn A/B tests have no extra fee, but LinkedIn suggests a $5,000 test budget. WhyUser is 50 credits per creative, about $222 on the Scale plan. Checked 7 November 2026.

What should I use to compare ad concepts without buying traffic?

Use a test that does not need media spend. WhyUser runs each ad and its landing page past 300 simulated buyers and shows which version wins by role, and why. A panel such as Wynter adds real quotes. Spend the budget on the version that survives.

What ad testing software do marketers recommend for reaching technical buyers?

WhyUser models the tech lead and security reviewer alongside the champion, budget owner and end user, and follows each one onto the landing page. Wynter can recruit real professionals by role. For live tests, LinkedIn targets by job title, function and seniority.

Who offers ad creative testing for small marketing teams?

PickFu is the cheapest way to poll consumers on ad options. Wynter is pay per test with no subscription. WhyUser runs a free 5-week design partnership for B2B teams, then plans start at $1,000 a month.

Why test the landing page with the ad?

Because B2B ads often lose the buyer after the click. In a published WhyUser sample run, 134 of 300 simulated impressions clicked, but the page did not deliver the guide the ads promised: 81 left at the page and 52 at the form. A test of the ad alone would have called it a winner.

What's a good alternative to focus groups for testing ad copy?

Test the ad and its landing page together on a simulated buying committee, then ask real buyers about the final version. Focus groups take weeks and cost five figures. See the alternatives to focus groups for a full comparison.
