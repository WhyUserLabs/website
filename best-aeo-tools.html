How to choose

Four questions pick the tool.

Do you know what buyers ask?

If not, start there. A tracker only watches the prompts you give it, and only the first turn.

Do you need a daily score?

Pick one tracker: HubSpot AEO is cheapest, and Profound covers the most engines.

Who writes the fixes?

For many pages at once, AirOps. For each answer drafted from your facts and tested first, WhyUser.

What will you report?

Coverage and AI accuracy are numbers your CMO can check. A visibility score alone is hard to act on.

Use them together

Find. Fix. Test. Track.

  1. FindWhyUser asks AI your buyers’ questions, follows up turn after turn, and finds where a rival answers instead.
  2. FixDraft each answer from your facts and put it on the right page.
  3. TestRun the page past your buying committee before it goes live.
  4. TrackLoad the questions into your tracker. Watch coverage, accuracy and visibility move.
Common questions

Answers, briefly.

What is the best AEO tool for B2B?

It depends on the job. To find which buyer questions your pages miss, grade AI’s answers against your facts and test each fix, WhyUser. For a low-cost daily visibility score, HubSpot AEO. For enterprise tracking across up to 10 AI engines, Profound. To write and refresh content at scale, AirOps. Many B2B teams pair WhyUser with one tracker.

What is the difference between an AEO tracker and buyer question coverage?

A tracker counts how often AI names you on a set of prompts. Buyer question coverage starts from the questions your buyers really ask, checks whether your own pages answer each one, and shows the rival page that answers instead. One tells you the score. The other tells you what to write.

How much do AEO tools cost?

HubSpot AEO starts at $50 a month for 25 prompts. Peec AI starts at $95 a month for 50 prompts. Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit is $99 a month per domain. Scrunch Core is $250 a month for 125 prompts. Profound and AirOps do not publish prices. WhyUser starts at $1,000 a month on an annual contract, with a free gap list for one product. Checked 23 November 2026.

Which AEO tools check whether AI’s answer is right?

Trackers show what AI says, with sentiment and citations. None of the trackers we checked says on its own pages that it grades the answer against your facts. WhyUser does, and shows fixed questions next to the ones not fixed yet. Checked 23 November 2026.

Which AEO tools test follow-up questions?

WhyUser does. It runs multi-turn chats with AI: a persona asks, reads the answer and follows up for 3 to 5 turns, and WhyUser checks your site at each turn. None of the other tools on this list says on its own pages that it does this. Checked 23 November 2026.

Does WhyUser work with my AEO tool?

Yes. WhyUser finds the buyer questions that matter and the ones a rival answers. Load them into HubSpot AEO, Profound, Peec AI or your tracker as prompts, so it watches what buyers really ask.

Which AEO tool writes the content?

AirOps runs content workflows at scale. Profound has agents for writing. HubSpot AEO has content tools in Marketing Hub Pro and Enterprise. WhyUser drafts each answer from your facts, places it on the right page and tests it on your buying committee before you publish.

What should a B2B marketing team report to show AEO is working?

Two numbers your CMO can check. Coverage: how many of your buyers’ top questions your pages now answer, counted by week 2. Accuracy: how often AI gets your facts right on fixed questions versus questions not fixed yet, by week 5. Your tracker adds the visibility score.
