How a question moves

Every question climbs five steps.

Where a question stops tells you what to do next. Each step is counted or checked, never guessed.

  1. Asked

    A buyer’s exact words, with source and date.

  2. Counted

    One per person, from the last 12 months.

  3. Listed

    Ranked by who asks, stakes, gap and trend.

  4. Answered

    A sentence on your page answers it.

  5. Backed

    You have proof on file.

  • Stops at Asked or CountedWait. It is not on your list yet.
  • Stops at ListedWrite the answer.
  • Stops at AnsweredGet proof first.
  • Reaches BackedDone, until buyers ask it differently.

Common questions

Answers, briefly.

Where do the questions come from?

Three places: your own calls, files your team uploads (CRM notes, RFPs, security questionnaires), and what buyers ask in public forums, Q&A threads and reviews. Each question keeps the buyer’s exact words, the source and the date, and counts different people, not pages.

How does WhyUser know if my pages answer a question?

It reads your key pages and marks each question answered, partly answered or not answered, with the sentence from your page that answers it, checked in code. It also shows which rivals’ pages answer it, whether you have proof on file, and one fix.

Can I use Buyer Questions for AEO and GEO?

Yes. Filter to questions that name no vendor. Those are the questions buyers put to ChatGPT, Gemini and Google. Answer them on your pages in plain words, then track them in your AI visibility tool.

How is this different from Buyer Personas?

Personas describe who decides and what they need. Buyer Questions lists what they actually ask, ranked by role and stage. Both feed every simulation.

Are quotes from my calls shared?

No. Quotes from your calls stay in your workspace. Public quotes are counted, never republished.

What if no buyer has asked a question yet?

WhyUser can write a likely question from what your buyers already say. It is labeled Compiled, ranked no higher than Medium, and never counted as asked until a real buyer asks it.
