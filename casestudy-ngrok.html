Could Audience Discovery see the audience ngrok already knew?

06 · Live in Apollo

The five emails went live together. Then ngrok narrowed the field.

Early live signal · test in progress 2/5

ngrok sent all five in Apollo, its sales email tool. Two of the five ideas moved forward to the next round, based on early opens and clicks. The test is still running.
