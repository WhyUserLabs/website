Customer story

Six questions ngrok answered before a buyer ever saw the campaign.

A missing buyer role, a new HealthTech audience and a HIPAA blocker. ngrok found all three before launch.

Outcomes
  • Gaps detected1missing buyer role: Product Manager
  • Velocity improvement4 weeks→ to hoursto a testable buying committee

The journey

Six questions, in the order ngrok asked them.

Each answer started as a simulated buyer signal. ngrok checked each signal against Sales, real inbound or its own proof, then decided what to change.

Six questions
01

Does WhyUser see what ngrok already knows?

Before a new market, ngrok tested WhyUser on one it knew well. ngrok knew the four core roles for its Site-to-Site use case, so its existing landing page became the benchmark.

Answer: mostly
3/4

Audience Discovery found three of the four known core roles. ngrok Sales asked, “What’s missing?” Product Managers.

02

Why was one role missing?

Because the page never spoke to them. Sales confirmed the Site-to-Site page did not speak to Product Managers. ngrok updated the messaging and ran Audience Discovery again.

Before
3/4
After
4/4

Product Manager surfaced. With every known role found, ngrok was ready to point Audience Discovery at a new market.

03

How fast can ngrok reach a new vertical?

Next, ngrok kept the Site-to-Site use case and changed the lens to HealthTech, a vertical ngrok was expanding into.

Before
4 weeks

to find a new audience by hand: research, enrich, group roles, find patterns, validate with Sales.

With Audience Discovery
Hours

to a testable HealthTech buying committee.

04

Who is in the new audience?

The HealthTech run surfaced more than 100 related roles. ngrok Sales reviewed the roles and validated them.

One example
Clinical Informatics

The role matched ngrok’s real healthcare inbound. A senior clinical informatics leader there had already asked about BAAs.

Those roles, plus ngrok’s inbound, became the HealthTech outbound audience built in Clay.

05

Would that committee believe the email?

Instead of more rounds of internal review, five people wrote five ideas: Marketing Ops, Sales, Sales, CRO, Product Growth. All five faced a simulated committee built from the HealthTech roles WhyUser found, before anything went live.

The committee pushed back
“Can I verify the HIPAA claim?”
Fixed before launch
Direct link to HIPAA reports in the Trust Center
06

What happened when the emails went live?

ngrok launched the five emails together as cold outbound tests in Apollo. So far, two of the five have moved forward to the next round.

Early live signal · test in progress
2/5

Two ideas moved forward to the next round.

Every WhyUser signal on this page is simulated. Where this page says a signal matched, ngrok checked that signal against real data it already had. The live results are ngrok’s, not WhyUser’s.

Epilogue · and the next question

The offer changed. So ngrok reworked both emails and asked the committee again.

The learning loop kept going. WhyUser brings buyer evidence earlier. ngrok adds context and decides what to apply.
