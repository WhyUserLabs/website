Common questions

Answers, briefly.

How long does a landing page test take, and what does it cost?

About 30 minutes. A page test fields 50 simulated buyers, ten for each of the five committee roles, and costs 20 credits. On the Scale plan that is about $89 a page. Pricing.

Do I need live traffic or recruited users?

No. A run needs only a URL, staging included, or a PDF or pasted copy. No tag, no traffic and no panel.

Which pages can I test?

Landing, product, pricing and solution pages, and content pages: blog posts, white papers, case studies and docs. Each runs the same committee.

What is a handoff, and why does it matter?

B2B buyers pass the case to each other: the end user to the champion, the champion to the budget owner. Deals often stall in that pass, even when every role likes the page. WhyUser scores each handoff, so you see which one breaks and why.

How is this different from a heatmap or an A/B test?

Heatmaps and A/B tests need traffic and arrive after launch. They show what happened, not which role left or why. WhyUser runs before launch and names the role, the line and the missing proof. Pre-launch testing vs A/B testing.
