What makes WhyUser different

Four things a measurement has to do, and two things B2B needs.

Ask any tool on this page, including us.

01 · Repeats

Same page in, same answer out.

Fix one section, re-run, and only that section can move. So you know whether your fix worked.

02 · Shows its source

Every quote traced to a real page.

Checked against the page it came from, and you are told who wrote it.

03 · Reads like your buyer

Scans, hunts, abandons. Never patient.

Distracted, skeptical and ideal, with motivation, familiarity and skill mixed to match your channel.

04 · Remembers

Your 10th run is better tuned than your 1st.

Every finding graded HIT or MISS against what happened, and the result carries forward.

05 · Simulates the committee

B2B buyers decide together.

Roles are computed as a graph, not averaged, so one blocking role sinks the verdict.

06 · Buyers stay current

Buyers are not files. They are feeds.

Each one re-compiles from live evidence on every run, so when your market moves, they move.
