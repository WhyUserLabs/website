The one-screen version

Every method below answers a different question. The mistake that costs the most is not picking the wrong tool — it is picking a tool for a question it was never built to answer, then concluding the method does not work.

Where WhyUser is the wrong choice

Stated plainly, because you will find out anyway.

  • You need a quote from a real human to settle an internal argument. Use a panel.
  • Your problem is inside the product — onboarding, workflows, dashboards. Use usability research.
  • You have the traffic volume and the time to reach significance, and the question is purely which variant wins. Run the A/B test.
  • You sell B2C or e-commerce. Buying committees are shallow there, so committee evidence carries less weight and the maths does not work for you.
