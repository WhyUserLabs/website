Common questions

Answers, briefly.

What does an email test cover?

Up to five emails, read in order. Each role sees the subject and preview, then the body, then the page each link opens. You see opens, clicks, replies and meeting requests per role, and where each one stopped.

What does it cost?

20 credits per combination of one subject, one body and one link, with 300 simulated buyers, on the Scale plan and above. Three subjects against two bodies is six combinations. Pricing.

Can it predict my reply rate?

No. The rates are directional. When a run records few replies, the report says so and tells you not to read the rate as a forecast. What it does show is which email, line and ask loses which role.

Does it work for cold outbound sequences?

Yes. The sample run is a five-email cold sequence from an SDR: a cold open, three value emails and a break-up. It found the same meeting ask repeated in three emails with no new proof.

Do I have to send anything?

No. Nothing is sent to anyone. The emails are read by simulated buyers built from your buyers’ own words.
