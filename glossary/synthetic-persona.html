Synthetic persona

Also called: simulated buyer, AI persona, agent persona.

Definition

A synthetic persona is a simulated buyer compiled from real evidence — product reviews, sales-call transcripts, community threads, competitor comparisons — and then run as an agent that reads material and makes decisions. It differs from a traditional marketing persona, which is a static document nobody opens, and from a generic AI prompt persona, which is invented at the moment of asking and forgotten immediately after.

Three things called “persona”

Built fromA workshop and some interviewsThe sentence you just typedReviews, calls, community threads, competitor gaps
FormA slide or a PDFA paragraph in a chat windowAn agent that reads and decides

The useful distinction is not “is it AI.” It is whether the persona was compiled from evidence or invented on the spot, and whether it produces a decision you can act on or a description you file.

What “compiled from evidence” means in practice

A synthetic persona is only as honest as its inputs. WhyUser compiles from six:

  • Company context — the site, the ACV, the motion.
  • Customer voice — call transcripts, recorded objections.
  • Market intel — community discussion, competitor gaps.
  • AI awareness — how AI assistants currently describe the category.
  • Campaign context — the promise made, the destination.
  • Live outcomes — what actually happened last time.

Every finding a persona produces cites the evidence behind it. That is the difference between something you can forward to a sceptic and something you have to defend.

The honest limits

Synthetic personas are frequently oversold, so it is worth being blunt about what they cannot do.

  • They are not a substitute for talking to customers. They are compiled from customer evidence, so they are downstream of it. Thin evidence gives you a confident-sounding simulation of nothing.
  • They predict rank and direction, not absolute rates. A/B testing is still the only thing that measures live performance.
  • They inherit the bias of the corpus. Public reviews skew toward the delighted and the furious. The quiet middle is under-represented, and a good system says so rather than smoothing it over.
  • A single loud quote is not a pattern. It should stay an outlier until other buyers confirm it.

Questions people ask about synthetic persona

What is a synthetic persona?

A simulated buyer compiled from real evidence such as reviews, sales-call transcripts, and community threads, then run as an agent that reads material and makes decisions rather than sitting in a document.

Are synthetic personas accurate?

They are accurate at rank and direction rather than absolute numbers, and only as good as the evidence they were compiled from. The honest way to judge a vendor is to ask whether they publish a graded track record of predictions sealed before the outcome existed.

How is a synthetic persona different from asking ChatGPT to act as a buyer?

A prompt persona is invented at the moment of asking, produces a different answer each time, and cites nothing. A synthetic persona is compiled from a fixed evidence base, returns the same verdict for the same input, and sources each finding.

Do synthetic personas replace customer interviews?

No. They are built from customer evidence, so they sit downstream of it. If the underlying evidence is thin, the simulation is confident and empty.
