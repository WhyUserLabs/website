Before you ask

Questions we hear every week.

What is WhyUser?

WhyUser is the buyer simulation platform for B2B marketing. Buyer Questions Coverage shows what buyers ask AI, which questions your pages answer, and which ones a rival answers. It works with your AEO tool. Buyer & Committee Simulations show which buyer role stalls on each page. Ad Funnel Simulation shows who clicks your ad and who leaves the page it lands on.

We already use an AEO tool.

Keep it. Load the questions that name you as its prompts. It runs the checks, and WhyUser tells you what to fix.

Are the simulated buyers real?

No. They are a simulated buying committee, built from your market research, rivals and calls. Their questions stay labeled simulated until a real buyer asks one. When customers graded them, 84% of 473 findings held up.

What happens after the first answer?

Personas keep asking AI, for up to 5 turns. WhyUser checks your site at every turn, and shows where a rival takes over. The AI answers are real. The persona questions are simulated and labeled.

Will this raise our AI citations?

On questions that name you, likely: AI reads your own pages first. We show it on fixed vs not yet fixed questions by week 5. On questions that name no vendor, no one can promise it. We track it with you.

Can’t I just ask ChatGPT?

You’ll get a list. Not which questions your live pages miss, or which rival answers each one, with the quote.

We have no one to write.

WhyUser drafts each answer from your facts. Your writer edits, and you approve.

Does it test ads?

Yes. Ad Funnel Simulation shows your LinkedIn or Google ad to your buying committee in the feed, then follows each click to the landing page. You see who clicks, who stays and who leaves.

What does it cost?

The gap list is free: the top 10 questions a rival answers and you don’t, for one product. Plans start at $1,000 a month on an annual contract. Pricing · ROI calculator.
