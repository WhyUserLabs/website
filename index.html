01 · Know your buyer

Personas compiled from what your buyers say.

You don’t write them. Each buyer is compiled from six sources, before and after they talk to you, and recompiled as your data changes. Where sources disagree, a real buyer’s words beat the AI’s guess.

Six evidence sources compile into your buyer agents. Three are public and work from the first run: company context from your site, ACV and GTM motion; market intelligence from reviews, communities and competitors; and AI awareness of how ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity describe you. Three are yours and start when you connect them: customer voice from call recordings and CRM objections; institutional knowledge from win/loss notes, positioning and battlecards; and expert knowledge from experiment findings and the corrections your team makes. The agents recompile as your data changes.

Before you ask

Six questions we hear every week.

What is WhyUser?

WhyUser is pre-launch buyer simulation for B2B marketing teams. It builds buyer personas from your calls, reviews and buyer questions, then runs them as a buying committee on your pages, ads and emails. In about 30 minutes you see who drops off, why, and what to fix.

How does WhyUser build buyer personas?

From evidence, not a workshop. Public sources work from your first run: your site, reviews, community threads, rivals and how AI assistants describe you. Your calls, CRM objections and win/loss notes sharpen them when you connect them. Every trait cites its source. How personas are built.

Can it predict buyer objections before launch?

Yes. Five roles read your page in three moods and report which role stalls, on which line, and what proof was missing. Teams grade every finding against what really happened: 84% of 473 sealed claims held up.

Can I test a landing page with no traffic and no recruited users?

Yes. A run needs only a URL, a PDF or pasted copy, staging included. No tag, no traffic, no panel. A page test fields 50 simulated buyers and returns in about 30 minutes.

How is WhyUser different from Wynter or ChatGPT?

Wynter asks real people what they think, each on their own, in 12 to 48 hours. But what people say and what they do are not the same. ChatGPT is one careful reader with a new answer each time. WhyUser simulates how the whole committee behaves on your page, scores the handoffs between roles, and gives the same page the same verdict. vs Wynter · vs ChatGPT and Claude.
