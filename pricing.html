What you are buying

A run is not one opinion. Compare the depth, then the price.

Every run fields a full committee, with many agents per role, in three moods. It reports the spread, not an average.

50 to 300 agents

A page test fields 50 agents. Ad and email runs field 300.

Three behavioural states

Every role reads distracted, sceptical and ideal. Most of your traffic is not ideal.

The handoffs, not just the seats

We score the seven handoffs between five roles. That catches the deal that dies even when every role approves.

Replayable, not re-rolled

Same inputs, same buyers. Re-run after a fix and the change is yours, not model drift.

The four questions to ask when comparing. How many agents, how many moods, how many roles together, and does the same input give the same result twice?

Pricing FAQ

The questions procurement asks.

01What exactly is a credit?

The unit every run is priced in. Page tests, email combos and competitor intelligence runs are 20 credits; ad creatives and Audience Discovery runs are 50.

02Do credits expire, or do I have to spread them evenly?

There is no monthly cap, so spend them any time in your term. Credit packs never expire while you are subscribed.

03Why is there no monthly plan?

Plans are annual. If a year is too big a first step, the design partner programme is free.

04What happens when I run out of credits?

Nothing auto-charges and nothing breaks. Add a pack at $1,000 for 200 credits, or move up a plan and we credit what you have paid.

05Does a failed or useless run still cost credits?

A run that errors is not charged. If a finished run is unusable, say the wrong page went in, tell us and we return the credits.

06Do we pay per seat?

No. Seats are unlimited on every plan.

07How does this compare to a research panel?

A panel study costs $2,000 to $8,000 and takes hours to weeks. A WhyUser run costs $89 to $100 and takes about thirty minutes; see the full comparison.

08What does procurement need from us?

Our DPA and security docs are open to read before you send us a URL. Enterprise adds SSO, a custom DPA and security review support.
