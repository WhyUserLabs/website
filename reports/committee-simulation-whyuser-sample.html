What the committee believed that the page did not answer

The page assumes
If the value prop is clear and people can click to learn more, the buying committee will self-serve proof and progress to access.
What the run recorded
Committee verdict: 10/10 clicks and 0 abandons across CH/EB/EU/GR/TD, but repeated friction cites missing proof/trust signals and that CH can’t justify the decision to EB without ROI evidence; social proof is “thin/unnamed” early (wb_6d6ab656).
Cost
High-intent evaluators move forward but stall in internal approval: Champions lack “send-this-to-my-EB” proof, turning fast interest into slower cycles, more sales time, and higher deal slippage to tools with clearer ROI/case evidence.
What closes it
Add above-fold ROI + proof pack: 1–2 quantified outcomes, 2–3 named customer logos/case snippets, and a “business case” link/one-pager designed for EB forwarding before any additional CTA experimentation.

Medium severity · 6 flagged of 50 runs · reacted: CH · EB · EU · GR · TD

The page assumes
Repeating “Request access” and offering multiple actions increases conversions by meeting users wherever they are.
What the run recorded
Multiple roles reported competing actions and repeated “Request access” variants, blurring the recommended next step and increasing hesitation/misclick risk (wb_1649639e). This is especially costly when the page already drives clicks but needs stronger conviction signals.
Cost
More noisy clicks, fewer clean conversions: Economic Buyers and Gatekeepers defer or bounce to “learn more” paths, pushing more prospects into sales back-and-forth and lowering form-start rate quality.
What closes it
Collapse to one primary CTA per intent: (1) “Get ROI proof / Business case” for EB/CH and (2) “Request access” as secondary; remove duplicate CTA variants and standardize CTA labels sitewide to reduce cognitive load.
