Why no CTR or CVR

The roles that decide are the smallest slice of your traffic. Fix them and your page numbers do not move.

Economic Buyer 7% of your traffic Lift that slice 2% → 3% 3,800 sessions per arm = 2 years Blended, that same fix reads 2.00% → 2.07% To see 2.07% at all 600,000 sessions per arm = never

And analytics cannot tell you which sessions were theirs.

So we count what you can count. We never claim a lift.

How each number is built
Pages & content · counted(rounds today − rounds after) × hours saved × marketing rate × assets/yr × your confirmed rate
What it costs in team time to get one asset through review, before and after.
Learning cycles · countedhours saved × cycles/yr × marketing rate × your confirmed rate
The work of finding out whether a shipped asset is working. No overlap with the review line: that one is the argument before a page ships, this one is the cycle after it.
Two things are deliberately absent. The wait, because nobody sits idle while traffic accumulates, so calendar time never becomes money — it is on line 4, in days. And the run's own cost, because the plan price is already the denominator of the ratio; deducting it here would charge for it twice.
Ads · protectedspend/mo × (flagged ÷ checked) × (days to next review ÷ 30) × campaigns/yr × your confirmed rate
The committee run says what the page answers, the ad run says what the creative promised, and we count the gap.
No overlap with the pages line. That one counts marketing hours before launch, this one counts paid clicks after it.
Email · counted(reviewers × hours × rounds removed × marketing rate + copywriter × rounds removed) × sends/mo × 12 × your confirmed rate
