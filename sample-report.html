Anatomy

What every report gives you.

Same shape whichever run you open. A verdict per role, the handoff that breaks, and a queue you can hand to whoever does the work.

01 · The verdict

Where each role landed, and why.

Every role that has to approve, scored on its own. Convinced, blocked, or somewhere between, with the line that decided it.

02 · The conflict graph

Which handoff snaps.

Roles pass the deal to each other. The graph shows which pass fails. This is the failure an average score cannot see.

03 · Working and broken

Both sides, with counts.

The lines that landed and who they landed with, next to the ones that lost people, with how many buyers each affected.

04 · The fix queue

Ranked by handoffs unblocked.

Not ranked by page order. Each fix names the element to change and how many buyers it frees.

Every finding traces back to its source in Ground Reality, so you see why a buyer stopped rather than only that they did.

Why it reads handoffs

All five convinced. Nobody could hand it on.

In the committee run against whyuser.com, all five roles finished convinced at intent 7 out of 10. Forty-five of fifty passes clicked forward. None converted, because two of seven handoffs snapped: the End User had no docs for the Technical Decision Maker, the Champion had no proof for the Economic Buyer. No single role failed. An average would have called the page a winner.
