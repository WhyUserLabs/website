Common questions

Answers, briefly.

Are these real reports?

Yes. Each one is a full WhyUser report from a design-partner or customer run, unedited. Company names and links are replaced with Acme Corp.

Why are they anonymized?

A page’s weak spots, a campaign’s click data and a targeting map belong to the customer. So the company is shown as Acme Corp.

Are the clicks, opens and replies real?

No. They are simulated buyers, built from real buyers’ words. Simulated buyers click and open more often than real ones, so each report judges a role against an average in the same simulation. Read them as direction, not as rates.

How long did each run take?

About 30 minutes for a page, ad or email run, from a URL or the copy. Buyer questions and market trends are refreshed on a schedule.

Can I get one on my own page?

Yes. Request access and we run your next launch: a page, an ad or an email sequence. Apply here.
