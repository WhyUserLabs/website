Solution

Find. Fix.
Test. Track.

One loop. Your first number in two weeks.

  1. 01 · Week 0

    Find

    WhyUser pulls buyer questions from data before buyers talk to you, like search, AEO prompts, market research and rival reviews. And from data after, like sales calls and your CRM. Each question shows who asks it, and who answers it: you, a rival or no one.

  2. 02 · Weeks 1–2

    Fix

    WhyUser drafts each answer from your facts and places it on the right page. Your writer edits, and you approve.

  3. 03 · Staging for marketing

    Test

    Like engineering, test before you ship. Personas ask AI, then ask again. At each turn, WhyUser checks your site for the next questions.

  4. 04 · Weeks 2–8

    Track

    WhyUser counts the gaps you closed, then checks if AI gets your facts right, turn after turn. Fixed questions sit next to the ones you haven’t fixed yet.

Follow-ups

Buyers don’t stop at one question.
WhyUser doesn’t either.

A persona asks AI, reads the answer, and asks again, for 3 to 5 turns. At each turn, WhyUser checks your site for the next five questions.
