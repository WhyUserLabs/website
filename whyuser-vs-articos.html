What each one is for

Different jobs.
Sometimes both.

Articos gives you

  • Synthetic user research built on published behavioral science, with a stated accuracy figure.
  • Mixed stances: personas range from champions to skeptics and blockers.
  • Low entry price, and a 14-day trial with no card.
  • Its limits: each persona is interviewed alone, and it does not use your own records such as calls or CRM.

WhyUser gives you

  • The committee: five roles read the same asset, scored on the handoffs.
  • Buyers built from your evidence: calls, CRM objections, reviews and buyer questions.
  • Ranked fixes, then workflows that revise the page for you.
  • Its limits: simulated buyers, built for B2B marketing launches.

Common questions

Answers, briefly.

Is WhyUser an Articos alternative?

For testing B2B launches on a buying committee, yes. For quick, self-serve synthetic interviews about a message or concept, Articos is cheaper to start.

Does Articos simulate a buying committee?

Articos mixes stances across its personas, from champions to blockers, and interviews each one alone. WhyUser has five committee roles read the same page and scores the handoffs between them, where a B2B deal often stalls.

Which costs less?

Articos starts at $29 a month, with a 14-day trial and no card. WhyUser starts at $1,000 a month on an annual plan, with a free 5-week design partnership. Checked 7 November 2026.

Can both use my own data?

Articos does not require your documents; it builds personas from the context you describe or your website. WhyUser builds buyers from public evidence and, when you connect them, your calls, CRM objections and win/loss notes.
