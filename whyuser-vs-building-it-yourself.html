The honest part

You probably can build it.

Five role prompts, a loop, a scoring pass, a report template. It will look like our output by Sunday night.

A weekend gets you

  • Agents that read a landing page.
  • Personas holding different briefs.
  • Structured JSON coming back.
  • A report that reads well.

A weekend does not get you

  • A seal store, so an untouched finding replays instead of being recomputed.
  • A verifier, proving each quote exists in the page it cites.
  • Author classification, so a rival’s page is never read as a buyer’s words.
  • A graded record, sealed before outcomes exist.

Three things that break

None of them show up in a demo.

Drift

Your verdict moves when your page did not.

The same prompt can still return different text, so you cannot tell your edit from the noise.

Even splits

Simulated click rates come back at 20 to 30%.

An even mindset split over-represents the actively shopping buyer more than tenfold. Real cold LinkedIn is under 1%.

No handoff graph

Every role approves and the deal still dies.

Catching this means scoring the edges, not the roles. Most in-house builds stop at per-role output.

Common questions

Answers, briefly.

Can I build this with Claude Code and agents?

Probably. The orchestration is a weekend. The gap is everything after it.

What is the real cost?

Maintaining simulation infrastructure through every model update, for exactly one customer.

When should I build it?

When simulation is your product, or you have a research function that owns methodology.

Can I copy the calibration?

No. You can copy a sprint. You cannot copy a quarter of graded outcomes.
