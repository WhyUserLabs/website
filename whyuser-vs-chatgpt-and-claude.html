The objection

“But we would connect it to our own data.”

You can, and it helps. Attaching evidence is the easy part now. Collecting it is not. A search returns pages. What we run is a pipeline.

Reads, does not accumulate

It cannot tell you this week is last week.

Across four weeks of real buyer evidence, zero findings carried over between weeks. The themes were there every week, in different words.

No author class

A rival’s page and a buyer review are both text.

Point any agent at your category and much of what it finds was written by companies that sell against you.

Nothing is checked

A fluent quote and a real one look identical.

Cleaned, classified by author, and every quote checked against the page it came from. A search does none of that.

Check us

Two tests. Neither requires trusting us.

On Claude, four minutes.

Two fresh sessions, no shared history. Same page, same question. If the answers disagree, you have an opinion generator.

On us, one email.

Send three campaigns you already ran and know the results of. We predict the ranking. You check it.

Common questions

Answers, briefly.

Can I just use Claude to review my landing page?

For drafting and variants, yes. For a spend decision, no, because every run is a first run.

What if we use Claude Cowork with our data connected?

It reads your evidence well. It cannot tell you it read the same thing last week.

Is WhyUser just a wrapper around Claude?

The model is the cheapest part. The product is sealed re-runs, quote verification, author classification and a graded record.

Claude keeps getting better. Does this expire?

No. Fresh sampling is a feature for writing and a defect for testing, so the gap widens as Claude improves.

What does a committee add over playing five roles?

The handoffs. Five roles played in one pass agree with each other. Real committees do not.
