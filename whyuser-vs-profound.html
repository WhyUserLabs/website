WhyUser vs Profound

Profound tracks how your brand shows up across up to 10 AI engines, and runs agents that write. WhyUser starts from the questions your buyers really ask, grades whether AI gets your facts right, checks your live pages sentence by sentence, and tests each fix on your buying committee.

What each one is for

They watch AI.
WhyUser fixes your pages.

Profound gives you

  • Tracking across up to 10 AI engines, including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews.
  • Citations, sentiment, ranking and competitive presence in AI answers.
  • Prompt volumes with all-time history, exports and an API.
  • Agents and an AI Marketer for research, writing and reporting.
  • Agent Analytics: AI-sourced traffic, through Cloudflare, Vercel, Google Analytics and more.
  • Its limit: one Enterprise plan with custom pricing and a demo. Its pricing page does not say it checks whether your own page answers each buyer question.

WhyUser gives you

  • Your buyers’ own questions, from calls, CRM, Search Console and public research, each labeled with its source.
  • A sentence-level check of your live pages, with the rival page that answers instead and its quote.
  • AI answers graded against your facts, with fixed questions next to the ones not fixed yet.
  • Multi-turn chats with AI, with your site checked at each turn, so you see where a rival takes over.
  • A committee test before you publish.
  • Its limits: fewer AI engines, no traffic analytics, and simulated personas rather than real people.

Multi-turn chats with AI

Buyers don’t stop at one question.
WhyUser doesn’t either.

A prompt is one turn, but a buyer asks AI, reads the answer and asks again. WhyUser follows up for 3 to 5 turns, checks your site at each turn, and shows where a rival takes over.

Use both

One tracks the score. One tells you what to write.

  1. Find: WhyUser lists the questions your buyers ask, by role, and the ones a rival answers instead.
  2. Track: load the questions that name you into Profound as prompts, so its tracking follows what buyers ask.
  3. Fix and test: publish the answers WhyUser drafts, test them on your committee, then watch both numbers: coverage in WhyUser, visibility in Profound.
Common questions

Answers, briefly.

Is WhyUser a Profound alternative?

Not for tracking. Profound watches more AI engines, every day. WhyUser shows which buyer questions your pages miss, grades whether AI gets your facts right, and tests each fix. Many teams use both.

Did Profound drop its $99 plan?

Its pricing page says the Starter and Growth brand plans were retired in September 2026. Today it lists one plan, Enterprise, with custom pricing and a demo. Checked 23 November 2026.

Does Profound check whether AI’s answer is right?

Its pricing page lists citations, sentiment, ranking and competitive presence. It does not say it grades answers against your facts. WhyUser does, and shows fixed questions next to the ones not fixed yet.

Which costs less?

Profound does not publish a price. WhyUser starts at $1,000 a month on an annual contract, with a free gap list for one product. Checked 23 November 2026.

Does Profound test follow-up questions?

Its pricing page does not mention follow-ups. WhyUser runs multi-turn chats with AI: a persona asks, reads the answer and follows up for 3 to 5 turns, and WhyUser checks your site at each turn. You see the turn where a rival takes over. Checked 23 November 2026.

Can I use both?

Yes. WhyUser tells you what to write and tests it. Profound tracks visibility across more engines, and AI-sourced traffic.
