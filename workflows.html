The five workflows

One request does the whole job.

  1. Revise ContentWhen a run flags a page, blog post or case study.

    The revised content, plus an audit of which fixes it made. Ready for sign-off.

    WhyUser: test example.com/pricing with the Platform committee, then revise it from the fixes
  2. Revise Email SequenceWhen an email run flags a sequence.

    New subject lines, bodies, links and order of asks, plus the audit. Ready to test and send.

    WhyUser: test this sequence with the Platform committee, then rewrite it from the fixes
  3. Write Persona MessagingWhen you need to reach one buyer.

    What they care about, what to say and prove, what to avoid, objections with answers, and a draft per channel.

    WhyUser: write email messaging for the SRE who reads example.com/platform
  4. Find Common PatternsWhen you have several runs, such as every page since a positioning change.

    What keeps working, what keeps breaking, and the few fixes that solve the most.

    WhyUser: find common patterns across our last 10 runs, then apply them to example.com/pricing
  5. Compare Before and AfterWhen a page is read again after its fixes.

    What improved, what got worse, the fixes left, and who each version reaches.

    WhyUser: compare the new example.com/pricing with its last read
How a workflow runs

Read, change, check, save.

  1. 1 · ReadThe runs, fixes and buyer evidence behind them.
  2. 2 · ChangeThe page, sequence or messaging, in your voice rules.
  3. 3 · CheckEvery fix it made, and every one it did not.
  4. 4 · SaveIn WhyUser, ready for your team to mark.

Common questions

Answers, briefly.

Do I need to learn commands?

No. Start the request with WhyUser and name the page and the committee or persona. Claude picks the workflow.

Which tools does it work in?

Claude Code, the Claude desktop app and claude.ai, through the WhyUser skill and MCP server. Cursor and Codex connect to the same MCP server. Setup guide.

Is the output checked?

Yes. Every workflow ends with an audit of which fixes it made and which it did not. The run is saved in WhyUser so your team can mark it.

Who controls spend?

An admin sets each person to off, read only, or read and run, with a credit cap. The price shows before every run, and no key can delete anything.
